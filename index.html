Research

Four peer-reviewed papers on stochastic processes and simulation. The transferable core: modelling noise, validating models against data, and writing reproducible scientific code.

Soft Matter · 2025

Microrheological model for Kelvin–Voigt materials with micro-heterogeneities

A model for how microscopic irregularities shape the behaviour of gel-like materials, validated with simulations and fitted to experimental data from two real gels.

DOI
Journal of Physics: Condensed Matter · 2025

Heterogeneous response and non-Markovianity in the microrheology of semisolid viscoelastic materials

Simulations showing that hidden irregularities inside soft materials push measurements away from the usual Gaussian behaviour, plus an analytical way to detect them from the data.

DOI
Physica A · 2022

Time-correlated forces and biological variability in cell motility

A stochastic model of cell movement that accounts for memory in internal forces and cell-to-cell variability, with exact predictions confirmed by simulation.

DOI
Journal of Physics: Conference Series · 2020

Microrheology of filament networks from Brownian dynamics simulations

Brownian dynamics simulations that infer the mechanical properties of cytoskeleton-like filament networks from the random motion of embedded particles, reproducing key experimental features such as the sol–gel transition.
